Accuracy = 100 − ΔE (perceptual color distance, where 100 is black vs. white). 99%+ ≈ identical, 97%+ very close, 94%+ noticeably similar, below 90% = nearest we have. Screen colors are approximations — confirm on the physical book for critical jobs.
Thread colours come from Madeira’s own Polyneon shade card. Thread is shiny filament — sheen, stitch direction and density shift how a colour reads, more than ink does. Treat this as “pull these two or three cones” and let the physical card decide. Variegated shades (1510–1519, 1600–1609) can’t be scored and are left out, 1516 included.
