Accuracy = 100 − ΔE (perceptual color distance, where 100 is black vs. white). 99%+ ≈ identical, 97%+ very close, 94%+ noticeably similar, below 90% = nearest we have. Screen colors are approximations — confirm on the physical book for critical jobs.
Thread colours use reference RGB values for 343 Polyneon shades; the 1500-series ones marked est. have no published reference and are estimated from Madeira’s printed shade card — trust those least. Thread is shiny filament — sheen, stitch direction and density shift how a colour reads, more than ink does. Treat this as “pull these two or three cones” and let the physical card decide. Variegated shades (1510–1519, 1600–1609) can’t be scored and are left out, 1516 included.
